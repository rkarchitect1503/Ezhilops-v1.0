EZCloud Endpoint

Cloud assessment and optimization platform for AWS and Azure.

EZCloud Endpoint connects to your AWS accounts and Azure subscriptions with read-only access and gives you one clear picture of your cloud: what you have, how secure it is, what it costs, and how everything connects.

  • Inventory — every account and region in one view
  • Security — mapped to CIS Foundations Benchmarks
  • Cost — savings measured against real bills
  • Architecture — exposure, dependencies and traffic flows
Request a Demo

Unified Inventory

Discovers resources across all your AWS accounts, Azure subscriptions and regions into a single inventory.

Security & Compliance

Checks resources for security risks and maps results to CIS Foundations Benchmarks, alongside findings from Microsoft Defender for Cloud, Azure Policy, AWS Security Hub and Amazon GuardDuty.

Cost Optimization

Uses real billing data to show where your money goes, flag unexpected cost increases, and find idle, oversized or unused resources — then measures each saving against your bills.

Architecture & Network Insight

Shows what is exposed to the internet, which workloads depend on each other, single points of failure, backup gaps, and how traffic flows — including broken or misconfigured connections.

Prioritized Plan & Reports

One prioritized plan across security, cost and architecture, with clear reports for executive, security, finance and engineering teams.

Scheduled Assessments

Run assessments on a schedule and get alerted when new critical issues appear.

Read-only by design. EZCloud Endpoint never changes anything in your cloud.

Evidence for every finding. Every finding and saving comes with the evidence behind it.

No blind spots hidden. Anything it could not check is reported clearly, not assumed to be fine.
